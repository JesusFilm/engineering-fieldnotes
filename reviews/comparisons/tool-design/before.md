These are candidates for local correction, not grounds to replace its strong story.

A bounded query also does not itself guarantee complete coverage; no pagination is visible. C should remain complementary, with the claim boundary corrected or qualified.

These are scope limits, not automatic reasons to force all mechanisms into every cut.
