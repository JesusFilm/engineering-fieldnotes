## Guide relevance and primary-source audit

**Keep as reference**, matching `learning.placement`. A replicated tool service with measured uneven waiting is a real decision point for agent fan-out. A team starting with single-workstation delegation should learn tool contracts, bounded concurrency and evaluation first. The guide already gives a usable exercise: six fixed arrivals, single-slot FIFO workers, 200 ms and 2 s tasks, identical replay under both policies, wait/queue measurements, once-only completion and no migration.
