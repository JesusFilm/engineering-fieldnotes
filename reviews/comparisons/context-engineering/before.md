## Provisional comparative judgement

C is the strongest main chapter entry for a team beginning agentic engineering: it changes a concrete handoff/reverification practice and makes both a failure and its repair inspectable. B is a useful complementary explanation of compaction that retains constraints, after its numerical inconsistency and ambiguous success cue are corrected. A has the best incremental retrieval mechanism, but currently its precision-like timing, wrong-edit inference and overlapping badges weaken technical trust. Retain the complementary mechanisms rather than forcing the videos to teach identical stories.
